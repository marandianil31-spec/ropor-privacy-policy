Ropor Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

Last Updated: November 24, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Ropor. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the Ropor mobile application handles information when you use our random chat, photo sharing, and audio/video calling features.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, Ropor may process:

  • Guest identifiers: Anonymous session or device-related identifiers used to provide app features, if enabled in the app.
  • Chat information: Messages and information needed to deliver chat functionality.
  • Photos: Images you choose to share with another user.
  • Call information: Information needed to establish and manage audio and video calls.
  • Technical information: Device, application, network, and diagnostic information that may be processed by our service providers.

3. Camera and Microphone Permissions

Ropor may request access to your camera and microphone when you use video or audio calling features. These permissions are used to provide the calling features you choose to use. You can manage these permissions through your Android device settings.

4. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Provide random matching and chat services.
  • Send and receive messages and shared photos.
  • Establish and manage audio and video calls.
  • Maintain app security and prevent misuse.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the app.
  • Display advertisements where applicable.

5. Firebase Services

Ropor uses Google Firebase services, which may include Firebase Realtime Database, Firebase Storage, and Firebase Authentication or anonymous authentication, depending on the app configuration. These services help provide app functionality and may process information such as chat data, uploaded photos, anonymous identifiers, and technical information.

Firebase services are provided by Google and are subject to Google's privacy and security practices.

Learn more: Firebase Privacy and Security

6. Advertising

Ropor may use Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process certain device identifiers, advertising information, and usage information to provide, measure, and improve ads, subject to applicable settings and consent requirements.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed or shared with service providers such as Google Firebase and Google AdMob when necessary to operate the app, provide services, display advertisements, maintain security, or comply with legal obligations.

Information you choose to send to another Ropor user, such as chat messages or photos, may be visible to that recipient.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information processed through Ropor. Our service providers may use security measures such as encryption in transit. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the app's services, maintain security, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the service provider.

Ropor currently does not provide an automatic in-app account deletion button. If you want to request deletion of information associated with your use of Ropor, contact us at the email address below. Please include enough information for us to identify the relevant guest session or data.

We will review deletion requests and delete information we control where appropriate, subject to applicable legal requirements and legitimate retention needs. Some information held by service providers may be subject to their own retention policies.

10. Children's Privacy

Ropor is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, please contact us.

11. Third-Party Services

Ropor may rely on third-party services to operate certain features. Those services have their own privacy policies and data handling practices. We encourage users to review the policies of those services.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
